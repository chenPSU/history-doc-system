## Money facts used in planning

## Policy facts
- YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" on 15 Jul 2025; enforced at channel level from Jan 2026 (template titles/thumbnails, re-cut videos, mass-produced AI slop).
- AI disclosure: auto-labelling since 27 May 2026; realistic synthetic content must be disclosed.
- YPP: 1,000 subs + 4,000 long-form hours in 12 months until 31 Jan 2027 (23:59 UTC); 8,000 hours from 1 Feb 2027.

## Why automation still needs people
Research, fact-checking and narrative judgement keep the channel "authentic". The pipeline automates the repetitive production work; a person approves facts, voice, and every upload.
